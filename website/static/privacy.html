Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Public Beta Testing Stage • GDPR & CCPA Compliant

β Public Beta Testing Stage Notice

Notice: Connecto (including the connecto.fun web portal and official Android application) is actively operating in a Public Beta Testing Stage.

The platform is made publicly available for developer evaluation, performance profiling, and community feedback. While features, user interfaces, and network protocols undergo active optimization, our commitment to zero data selling, end-to-end cryptographic integrity, and data minimization remains non-negotiable.

01 Commitment to Privacy & Zero Data Selling

At Connecto Network ("Connecto"), privacy and cryptographic defense are fundamental engineering principles. We believe that real-time developer communication, audio streams, and collaborative workspaces must remain strictly confidential.

Core Guarantee: Connecto does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information, messages, profile data, or communication patterns to advertising networks, data brokers, or third parties under any circumstances.

02 Data We Collect

We practice aggressive data minimization. The only data processed includes:

  • Account Credentials: Chosen username, display nickname, optional bio/status, and securely hashed passwords (salted via Argon2id / bcrypt).
  • Channel Messages & Artifacts: Collaborative messages, code snippets, and emoji reactions sent in workspace channels.
  • Direct Communications: Encrypted message transmissions between authenticated participants.
  • Diagnostic Beta Telemetry: Ephemeral WebSocket ping metrics, packet round-trip time (RTT), and crash logs strictly utilized for network stabilization and bug remediation during the beta period.

We do NOT collect phone numbers, real-world physical addresses, or tracking identifiers.

03 Audio Lounges & Spatial Stages

Voice lounges and audio stages are powered by peer-to-peer WebRTC and high-throughput Coturn media relays:

  • Voice packets (96kHz Opus) are transmitted in real time and are strictly ephemeral in memory.
  • Connecto does not record, transcribe, store, or train machine learning models on voice stage conversations.

04 Cookies & Local Storage

Connecto does not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or behavioral cookies.

We strictly use secure, first-party browser local storage and essential session cookies for:

  • Keeping you securely logged into your authenticated account session.
  • Saving your UI preferences (dark mode OLED theme, audio volume levels, sidebar collapse state).

05 Infrastructure & Cryptographic Protection

All platform traffic is secured using TLS 1.3 encryption in transit with HTTP/2 and modern cipher suites (ChaCha20-Poly1305 and AES-256-GCM). Data at rest in SQLite write-ahead log (WAL) engines and Cloudflare D1 distributed edge nodes is stored on encrypted NVMe partitions.

06 Strict Age Requirement (18+)

Connecto and the Public Beta Testing Program are strictly designed for individuals aged 18 and older (or the legal age of majority in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly permit minors to register or collect data from individuals under 18 years of age. Accounts identified as belonging to underage individuals are terminated immediately.

07 Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under European GDPR and California CCPA regulations, you have full ownership of your data:

  • Right to Access: You can export your message archive and account profile data.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update or correct your username, bio, and credentials at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (Be Forgotten): Deleting your account permanently purges your profile, session tokens, and cryptographic keys from our database immediately.

If you have inquiries regarding our privacy practices, wish to submit an erasure request, or want to report a beta security finding, contact our team:
