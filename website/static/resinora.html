The Atelier Journey

Crafted with Intention in Machilipatnam

Resinora was born out of a simple, poetic desire: to suspend fleeting moments of nature's beauty in timeless, diamond-clear keepsakes. Founded by Meghana in Machilipatnam, Andhra Pradesh as a dedicated 100% online artisan atelier, Resinora bridges classical floral preservation with modern optical resin chemistry.

Operating purely online without walk-in physical stores, every piece is individually handcrafted to order right in Machilipatnam. Every flower is hand-foraged and carefully pressed in fine silica sand for 14 days to lock in its vibrant natural hue, vacuum-degassed to eliminate micro-bubbles, and carefully packaged before being shipped across India.

Whether it's a personal initial for daily keys, a bridesmaid suite for a wedding party, or a bespoke keepsake celebrating a loved one, we handcraft each piece as an heirloom meant to last decades.
